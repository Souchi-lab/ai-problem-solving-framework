# Goal

---

## Objective

Clarify the difficulty cards on the SoChi BLOCKS public site so that each level
communicates not only rough duration but also what kind of difficulty it
contains.

This follow-up should make puzzle selection easier for first-time and returning
users without redesigning the whole top page.

---

## Why This Follow-up

The public-site improvement report identified difficulty explanation as an
important UX gap:

- duration alone does not explain what makes a puzzle hard
- users need a quick reason to choose `Easy`, `Medium`, or `Hard`
- the top page should reduce hesitation, not ask users to infer too much

This is especially useful after the CTA and how-to improvements, because the
next point of hesitation is often:

- which difficulty should I start with?

---

## In Scope

- Improve the short copy shown with difficulty choices
- Add a brief explanation of "what is difficult" for each level
- Keep the change local to the difficulty-entry area

---

## Out of Scope

- Full top-page redesign
- Difficulty-system rebalance
- New recommendation engine
- Analytics / KPI work
- Viewer-side gameplay changes

---

## Success Criteria

1. The difficulty area becomes easier to choose from at a glance.
2. Each level communicates more than time estimate alone.
3. The copy stays short and selection-oriented.
4. The change remains local to the top-page difficulty section.
5. The result can be judged by reduced hesitation and clearer level meaning.

---

## Notes For Planner

The most important planning question is:

- what shortest copy best explains the kind of challenge at each difficulty

The planner should prefer:

- short, selection-helping text
- plain explanation over flavor text
- "why pick this level" wording rather than abstract labels

---

## Non-Goals

- Solving all onboarding friction at once
- Rewriting the whole puzzle list
- Mixing this with language-toggle or SEO work

---

## Desired Landing

At the end of this follow-up, the repo should have:

- a narrow top-page difficulty-copy improvement
- clearer reasons to choose each level
- a result that records whether the cards became easier to choose from
